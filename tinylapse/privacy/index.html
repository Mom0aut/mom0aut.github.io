TinyLapse Privacy Policy

Effective [27 November 2026] · App: TinyLapse (Android, app.tinylapse)

In short: your photos and everything you make with TinyLapse stay on your phone. There is no TinyLapse account, no cloud, no analytics and no ads. On Android the app has no permission to use the internet, so it cannot upload anything. The only thing involving a third party is buying TinyLapse Pro, which goes through Google Play.

Who is responsible

TinyLapse is made by Martin Waldl-Reinhart, Sorgenthalgasse 3/6/9. Contact: reinhart19_91@hotmail.com.

Because the app does not send your data to us, we do not hold or process any of your photos or personal data.

Photos you choose

  • You pick photos with your phone’s own photo picker or file picker. The app only gets the photos you select, never your whole library.
  • The chosen photos are copied into the app’s private storage on your phone. Your originals are never changed, moved or deleted.
  • From each photo the app reads only the date it was taken and its orientation, and uses nothing else, such as location. The copied photo files keep their original details (as they are in your library), and they stay in the app’s private storage on your phone.

Camera

The camera is used only on the “Take photo” screen, and a photo is taken only when you tap the shutter. These photos are added to your story on your phone and are not saved to your photo library. The app does not use the microphone.

Faces

To line your photos up, the app finds faces and compares them on your phone, using models built into the app. This face data is stored only on your phone, is never sent anywhere, and is deleted with the story. The face comparison data of other people in your photos is deleted once you have chosen who the story is about.

Videos and pictures you make

Videos, “then and now” pictures and collages are made on your phone. They are saved to your photo library or shared only when you tap Save or Share, and you choose where they go.

Other things stored on your phone

  • Story names, settings, an optional birth date (to show ages like “8 months”), music you add, and photo reminders.
  • Reminders are notifications scheduled by your phone itself. No push service or server is involved.

Buying TinyLapse Pro

  • TinyLapse Pro is a one-time purchase made through Google Play. Google handles the payment and your payment details; we never see them. Google’s own privacy policy applies to the purchase: policies.google.com/privacy.
  • To know whether you own Pro, the app asks the Google Play Store app on your phone. It keeps the answer on your phone (whether Pro is unlocked, Google’s purchase reference and when it last checked), so Pro also works offline.
  • Your photos and stories are never part of this.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No upload, cloud backup or sync.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, tracking or advertising.
  • No selling or sharing of data, because we have none to sell or share.

Deleting your data

Delete a story in the app to remove its photos, face data and videos. Settings → Delete all story data removes every story. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored. Videos or pictures you saved to your photo library stay there until you delete them yourself. A Pro purchase stays with your Google account and can be restored after reinstalling.

Children

TinyLapse is meant for adults, for example parents making a video of their child. It is not directed at children, and no data about anyone in your photos leaves your phone.

Your rights

Data protection laws such as the GDPR give you rights over your personal data. Since all data stays on your phone and under your control, you can see, change or delete it at any time in the app. For anything about this policy, contact [contact email].
